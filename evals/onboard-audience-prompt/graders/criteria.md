# Criteria: onboard-audience-prompt

Score each item independently. An item that cannot be checked from the output is a fail, not a pass.

- [ ] Asks whether the audience is internal or external
- [ ] States internal is the default
- [ ] Explains that external turns on a disclosure review and drops the unconfirmed-mentions block
- [ ] Asks the questions in a sensible order, a few at a time, not a wall of 15 questions
